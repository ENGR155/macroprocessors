Hardware

Candidate components and wiring for the magnetic levitation system.

Current Status

We have not selected, assembled, or tested the physical components. The parts below are candidates, and the bill of materials contains estimated allowances.

Course Boards

The design uses the STM32L432KC for position estimation and control, the UPduino v3.1 for sensor acquisition and current control, and the course ESP8266 for the webpage interface. The existing ESP8266 firmware is used unchanged.

Candidate Components

Function Candidate What we need to check
Magnetic field sensing Two TI DRV5055 Hall sensors Choose A3/A4 sensitivity based on the measured field range
Analog conversion MCP3204-CI/P 12-bit, 3.3 V operation with three channels
Coil current sensing INA240A1 and 0.100 ohm shunt Offset, gain, and usable current range
Gate driver UCC27511 Compatibility with the selected MOSFET and supply
Power switching MOSFET and flyback diode or clamp Coil current, supply voltage, and switching transients
Mechanical support Nonmagnetic frame and catch tray Repeatable gap and object orientation
Independent shutdown E-stop, current comparator, and output logic Shutdown delay and behavior when a connection is lost

With a 0.100 ohm shunt and a gain of 20, the ideal current-sense output is 2 V/A. A 3.3 V ADC reference gives approximately 0.403 mA per count. The actual offset and scale will need calibration.

Coil and Suspended Object

A coil’s holding-force rating at contact does not tell us how much force it can produce across an air gap. We plan to start with an instructor-approved test coil and measure force versus current and gap, winding resistance, inductance, and temperature.

The position estimate assumes that the object contains a permanent magnet with a repeatable orientation. We also need to check for rotation, attraction to the core, and gaps that produce similar Hall readings. A steel-only object would require a different characterization procedure.

Wiring and Initial Tests

The proposed netlist uses separate SPI buses for MCU-to-FPGA communication and FPGA-to-ADC acquisition. The hardware guide lists the jumpers, voltage levels, power connections, and interlock wiring.

The configuration values power_stage_ready=false, calibration_ready=false, and LEV_COMMISSIONED=0 keep the output disabled until the corresponding bring-up checks are complete.
