The course boards

The STM32L432KC handles position estimation and control. The UPduino v3.1 handles acquisition and current regulation. The existing course ESP8266 provides the wireless interface using its unchanged firmware.

Sensing and power

Function Candidate Selection constraint
Primary + reference magnetic field Two TI DRV5055 linear Hall sensors Evaluate A3/A4 sensitivity against the measured field range
Analog conversion MCP3204-CI/P 12-bit, 3.3 V; three channels used
Inline coil current INA240A1 + 0.100 Ω precision shunt Gain 20 candidate; calibrate offset and verify range
Gate drive UCC27511 Supply and drive must suit the selected MOSFET
Power switch + flyback path MOSFET and rated diode/clamp Choose from coil current, supply, and transient measurements
Mechanical system Nonmagnetic frame + catch tray Establish repeatable gap and object orientation
Independent shutdown E-stop, current comparator, output logic Validate propagation and fail-off behavior physically

At the example 0.100 Ω shunt and gain of 20, the ideal current conversion is 2 V/A. With a 3.3 V ADC reference, the ideal resolution is approximately 0.403 mA/count. Actual offsets and scale factors require calibration.

Coil and object come first

A holding-force rating at contact does not establish useful levitation force across an air gap. Start with an instructor-approved test coil and measure force versus current and gap, winding resistance, inductance, and steady temperature.

The proposed estimator assumes an object containing a permanent magnet with repeatable orientation. Rotation, attraction to the core, and ambiguous Hall readings are experimental risks. A steel-only object requires a different identification experiment.

Connectivity and commissioning

The proposed netlist documents two separate SPI buses: MCU-to-FPGA commands and FPGA-to-ADC acquisition. The hardware guide includes explicit jumpers, power domains, analog grounding, and interlock constraints.

Keep power_stage_ready=false, calibration_ready=false, and LEV_COMMISSIONED=0 until their corresponding bring-up gates pass.
