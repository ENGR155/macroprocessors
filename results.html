Results

Current assembly and bench progress, SPI development, historical checks, and remaining measurements.

Status - November 15, 2026

The printed setup is assembled, components have been received, Hall sensors are wired to the ADC and tested, and the electromagnet has been exercised with MOSFET-driven sweeping PWM. The current wiring puts ADC acquisition in the FPGA, with a separate MCU–FPGA SPI readout. The progress report includes the CAD iterations, code links, diagrams, and updated schematic.

Work Reported result Scope
CAD and fabrication Structure and mounts drafted, revised, printed, and assembled Physical test setup; CAD views included in the report
Hall sensors and ADC Sensor responses read at different magnet positions and data widths Raw magnetic-field-dependent readings; calibrated field/distance not established
Two-channel readout FPGA ADC/subtraction and MCU 16-bit reception code written End-to-end repeatability and representation still being debugged
MOSFET/PWM Electromagnet exercised with an up/down duty sweep, diode, and gate components Approximate supply-current maximum around 28 mA and power around 0.34 W reported during the sweep
Design review Basic presentation and revised block diagrams/schematic prepared Documents the current setup and intended integration

The supply observations are approximate bench reports, not rated coil measurements. Recent no-magnet ADC output varied substantially, so stable readout, framing, and noise remain active work. Stable levitation, measured position accuracy, continuous thermal performance, and shutdown latency have not yet been demonstrated.

SPI Bring-up — November 7

The project chat reported a received MCU value of 165, which equals 0xA5. This supports reception of the expected byte in the standalone FPGA-to-MCU test. The proposed second pattern, 0x3B (59), was a follow-up check; success has not been recorded.

The standalone guide records HDL simulation across all 256 bytes, MISO release on deselection, partial-byte restart, synthesis/routing using 8 logic cells, and Cortex-M4 compilation in Watch-only mode. These are recorded checks, not rerun measurements. They do not establish MOSI command reception, ADC sampling, DMA execution, the full application link, or levitation.

The required clock was 24 MHz. Tool timing is an estimate. The enabled build used nonzero test gains; shipped commissioning defaults remained disabled.

Calculations and Measurements

The proposal’s air-core sizing example cannot support 10 g at 20 mm center distance from a 12 V, 8 ohm coil. Its illustrative 10 mm position-PI model is unstable under the assumed inductance and damping. Neither calculation characterizes the selected electromagnet. See System Design.
