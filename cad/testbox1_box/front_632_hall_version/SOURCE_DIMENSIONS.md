# Native CAD dimensions and implementation

The revised geometry has been created and exported by the installed Autodesk Fusion native modeling engine. STEP, STL, SAT and a native F3D were produced during an actual Fusion API run. The separate OCP library was blocked by Windows Application Control and was not used or unblocked. These dimensions describe the delivered native geometry; the independent check report provides the exported-file verification results.

- Front rod center X90/Y40 before clamp seating; common Z111–248, upper and lower five-port zones retained.
- As-printed sanding stock: 13 × 11 × 137 mm. Intended finished target: 12.4 × 10.4 × 137 mm, seated 0.2 mm forward in Y.
- Upper guide: 12.8 × 10.8 mm. Lower guide: 13.2 × 11.2 mm. Upper guide intentionally smaller than the unsanded stock; the lower nominal guide is 0.2 mm larger overall than that stock.
- Finished clamped rod: X83.8–96.2, Y34.6–45.0. Sensor center X90/Y44.625; sensor faces remain horizontal.
- Front guide bosses: X79–101, Y30–50; upper Z190–200, lower Z162–170.
- Rod clamp: front-facing #6-32 × 3/4 inch (19.05 mm), Ø4.1 clearance; 8.3 mm across-flats captive nut track, 3.2 mm finished depth, 18.2 mm vertical length; slot 12.1 × 4.1 mm. Operating common movement ±4 mm; shaft/slot theoretical outer limit ±4.2974 mm.
- Revised cassette/head bolts: four #6-32 × 2 inch (50.8 mm), Ø4.1 holes at original 19 mm bolt-circle radius. Rear joint: four #6-32 × 1-1/4 inch (31.75 mm) through unchanged 4.5 mm holes with washers.
- Maximum washer envelope to check: Ø9.906 × 1.651 mm. Pan head envelope: Ø6.858 × 2.4638 mm. Nominal nut: 7.9375 mm across flats × 2.778125 mm thick; actual 03748 nuts still require measurement.
- Sensor case pocket: 4.3 mm wide × 1.9 mm high, 2 mm engagement after finishing. Native-pin bay: 3.8 mm wide × 1.9 mm high. Open +X wire-removal path: 3 mm high, through the right side over the whole rod depth. Removing a retaining loop permits the package and its attached flying leads to move sideways, while soldered joints remain outside the rod.
- Left spine at the wire openings: 4.05 mm finished width; nominal rod depth is 10.4 mm, locally reduced to 9.9 mm by the 0.5 mm-deep front loop-return relief. Between 3 mm-high open escape rows, the right region has 5 mm vertical webs at the 8 mm port pitch.
- Both fixed-magnet pockets retained: Ø19.6 × 7 mm, floors Z163 and Z193. Planned main assembly depicts two DC1 disks in each pocket, using the purchased total of four; pocket capacity remains four per location. Nonprinted retainer updated to 50 × 10 × 1 mm, Ø4.1 holes 38 mm apart.
