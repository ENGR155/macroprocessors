System Design

Current FPGA ADC acquisition, MCU SPI readout, PWM drive, and feedback integration.

Current Architecture

The November 15 bench setup uses two DRV5056 Hall sensors connected to an MCP3204 ADC, with ADC acquisition in the FPGA. The FPGA also generates a sweeping PWM signal for the electromagnet power stage. The STM32 reads the FPGA over a second SPI interface. The printed frame and mounts are assembled, and sensing and actuation have been tested as subsystems. See the progress report for the work since the proposal.

This supersedes the earlier MCU ADC/DMA architecture. The next integration step is reliable live readout followed by calibrated position estimation and feedback control. The final control law and operating rates still need validation on the assembled plant.

The proposal’s 5 kHz MCU acquisition and 1 kHz feedback figures are historical targets rather than rates measured on this wiring. Rebuild the timing budget around the FPGA ADC transactions, coherent measurement handoff, MCU exchange, and selected control update interval.

Gap and Position Control

The definitions and PI equation below record the proposal baseline. They describe a control approach to evaluate rather than a completed controller in the current sweep test.

Surface gap dd runs downward from the electromagnet’s bottom face to the suspended magnet’s top face. The proposal commits to 10–20 mm motion with a 10 g total moving mass. The force-model distance zz runs from the coil plane to the magnet center: z=d+Δgz=d+\Delta_g, where the offset depends on actual geometry.

For downward displacement, md̈=mg−F(d,i)m\ddot d=mg-F(d,i). Define e=d̂−re=\hat d-r, where rr is requested gap. The proposed controller is

u=clamp⁡(u0+Kpe+Ki∫edt,0,umax).u=\operatorname{clamp}\left(u_0+K_pe+K_i\int e\,dt,\;0,\;u_{\max}\right).

A positive error increases attraction. Duty limits, integrator anti-windup, range-checked gains, and applying both gains together at an update boundary are part of the implementation plan. Numeric gains require the measured plant and confirmed units.

Resolution and Calibration

Both Hall sensors see the electromagnet, fixed helper magnet, and moving payload. The current acquisition uses separate ADC channels and forms a difference in the FPGA. Two sensors alone do not guarantee coil-field cancellation. Record their individual responses and sensitivities before choosing the final estimator; no installed current-sense channel is established by the latest schematic.

For calibrated differential voltage VΔ(d)V_\Delta(d), the local quantization estimate is

ΔdLSB≈Vref/4096|dVΔ/dd|.\Delta d_{\mathrm{LSB}}\approx\frac{V_{\mathrm{ref}}/4096}{|dV_\Delta/dd|}.

At the proposed 3.3 V reference, one count is approximately 0.806 mV. Measure the voltage slope across the operating range. Noise, channel skew, alignment, and calibration also limit usable resolution and accuracy. The separate November sensing notes used a 5 V ADC example; their numerical position increments do not establish performance for this 3.3 V design.

A fixed helper magnet can change the equilibrium force and force gradient. Shifting the Hall baseline does not itself improve position resolution. Check sensor headroom and calibrate the complete assembly with the helper magnet installed.

Stability Calculation

The calculation summary develops the local model around equilibrium. Define x=d−d0x=d-d_0, j=i−i0j=i-i_0, and upward force F≃mg−kxx+kfjF\simeq mg-k_xx+k_fj. With resistance RR, inductance LL, passive damping cc, and supply VsV_s, position PI gives

mLs4+(mR+cL)s3+(cR−kxL+kf2)s2+(kfVsKp−kxR)s+kfVsKi=0.mLs^4+(mR+cL)s^3+(cR-k_xL+k_f^2)s^2+(k_fV_sK_p-k_xR)s+k_fV_sK_i=0.

The illustrative air-core model at 10 mm center distance, with 10 g mass, 500 turns, 10 mm coil radius, R=8ΩR=8\,\Omega, L=10L=10 mH, and negligible passive damping, has a negative s2s^2 coefficient. No PI gains make that assumed continuous model strictly stable. The sampled example is also unstable. These calculations do not validate gains for the selected iron-core electromagnet.

Measure holding current, hot resistance, inductance, and local force slopes using the final payload and helper magnet. Check continuous and sampled stability, including acquisition and communication delay, before free suspension. Stable poles and position tolerance are separate checks; the proposal’s endpoint demonstration band is ±5 mm.

Implementation Status

The current bench top connects ADC acquisition, measurement-word transmission, the duty sweep, and PWM. Written connections in RTL do not establish repeatable acquisition or closed-loop control. The MCU test reads 16-bit words for inspection.

The older full-application sources and October 26 verification cover MCU position control with FPGA current PI. They are retained as earlier evidence. Their packet contract, simulation results, and resource counts do not validate the current bench setup.
